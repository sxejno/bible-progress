How this works

1. Short rounds. Each round shows chapters due for review plus a few new ones. Grade yourself honestly — "Again" is not failure, it's scheduling.
2. Spaced repetition. Chapters you know come back at growing intervals (1, 3, 7, 16, 35, 75 days). Chapters you miss come right back.
3. Both directions. Seeing "Acts 5" and recalling the story is one skill; hearing the story and naming the chapter is another. Mixed mode trains both.
